# Domaine de Plaisance
A French pleasure palace and formal gardens to explore together in browser and VR.
Required: recognizable classical palace; gardens in front; central ornamental fountain with exactly six lateral curved jets and one taller central jet, visibly animated; real multiplayer rooms joinable by link, remote visitor avatars, disconnect/reconnect, shared fountain time.
[ASSUMED] Warm afternoon, cream limestone, slate roofs, symmetrical parterres, cypress avenues, roses, gravel paths. Exterior visit; no furnished interiors. Up to 12 visitors per room. No accounts or voice chat requested. Local build with deployable room server; Internet hosting not yet provisioned.
Platform: browser + Quest VR, built-in locomotion with walkable environment and clear spawn. Browser controls documented in French.
Loop: enter garden, walk to fountain, invite friends by room link, explore together.
Art: elegant architectural proportions, strong central axis, turquoise water, cream stone, muted greens, warm sunlight. Geometry procedural for control over architectural parts and exact jet count.
Success: native scene validates; palace and four parterres visible in hero and spawn views; seven WaterJet entities with six lateral and one central; animation changes with time; two clients in same room receive each other poses; other rooms isolated; departed avatars removed; typecheck/build clean.
